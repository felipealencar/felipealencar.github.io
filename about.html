I’m originally from Praia do Francês, in Marechal Deodoro, Alagoas (Brazil) — where I’ve called home since 2003. Life eventually brought me to Saint Louis, in the United States, where I live today.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from CIn/UFPE and I’m currently on leave from my faculty position at IFAL. I still believe the only reason I didn’t become a soccer player, surfer, or musician was lack of time (or perhaps talent?).

The truth is that I play well, catch good waves, and in computing the results are simply easier to document.

Academic and professional path

Then came my PhD at UFPE, my academic career at IFAL, the leadership of the Data Engineering and Analytics Lab (LEAD), and a period as a visiting researcher at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) in Germany.

Today

I work as a Research Scientist at Saint Louis University, focusing on artificial intelligence applied to environmental and geospatial problems — machine learning for agricultural monitoring, carbon sequestration estimation, and GeoAI.

This blog documents that journey: technology, AI, software engineering, and data science — but also what it means to live far from home, move to another country, and relearn everyday life. Between code and contemplation, with a daily longing for the sea.
